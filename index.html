Server & Casting Rules

§1. General Rules

  • Respect & Conduct: Zero tolerance for hate speech, harassment, toxicity, or discrimination. Treat everyone with respect.
  • No Spam/Promo: Do not post invite links or self-promotion outside of dedicated channels.
  • Privacy: Doxxing or sharing private DMs without consent results in an instant ban.

§2. Actor & Casting

  • Requirements: Clear microphone, Minecraft Java Edition, and high reliability for schedules.
  • Applications: Apply strictly in the designated casting channel. Do not DM staff about your status.
  • Strict NDA: Lore, scripts, plot twists, and behind-the-scenes plans are 100% confidential. Leaking results in a perma-ban.

§3. Filming & Voice

  • Audio Setup: Push-to-Talk is required in all recording and voice channels.
  • Follow Direction: Obey director calls during shoots. Intentionally throwing or ruining a shot gets you recast instantly.

§4. Enforcement

  • Staff Discretion: Staff reserves the right to mute, kick, or ban anyone violating these rules at any time.
